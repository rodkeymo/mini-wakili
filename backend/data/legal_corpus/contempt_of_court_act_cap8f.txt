# Contempt of Court Act (Cap. 8F) — Kenya (key excerpts)

1. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Contempt of Court Act.

Objects
The Act provides for the punishment of contempt of court in order to uphold the dignity and authority of the courts, ensure the due administration of justice, and protect the rule of law.

Civil contempt
A person who wilfully disobeys a judgment, decree, order, or other process of a court, or who wilfully breaches an undertaking given to a court, commits civil contempt and may be punished by the court.

Criminal contempt
Conduct that scandalises the court, interferes with judicial proceedings, or obstructs the administration of justice may constitute criminal contempt and is punishable under the Act.

Procedure
The Act sets out the procedure for initiating contempt proceedings, the rights of the alleged contemnor (including the right to be heard), and the powers of the court to impose sanctions including fine, imprisonment, or both, subject to the limits prescribed.

Limitation
Proceedings for contempt must be brought within the time limits prescribed by the Act. The Act also provides for the protection of fair comment and for the defence of truth in appropriate cases.
