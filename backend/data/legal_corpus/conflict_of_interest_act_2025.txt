# Conflict of Interest Act (No. 11 of 2025) — Kenya (key excerpts)

1. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Conflict of Interest Act, 2025.

Object
The Act provides a framework for the identification, disclosure, management and prevention of conflicts of interest by public officers and other persons to whom the Act applies, in order to promote integrity, transparency and accountability in public life.

Application
The Act applies to public officers, state officers, and other categories of persons as specified, including those exercising public functions or holding positions of trust.

Duty to avoid conflict of interest
A person to whom the Act applies shall not place himself or herself in a position in which his or her private interest conflicts with his or her public duty, or in which he or she may reasonably be perceived to have such a conflict.

Disclosure
A person to whom the Act applies shall disclose any actual, potential or perceived conflict of interest in the manner and within the time prescribed, and shall take such steps as are required to manage or eliminate the conflict.

Prohibited conduct
The Act prohibits the use of public office for private gain, the improper acceptance of gifts or benefits that could influence the performance of official duties, and participation in decisions where a conflict of interest exists, unless the conflict has been properly managed in accordance with the Act.

Enforcement
Breach of the Act may attract administrative sanctions, civil consequences, or criminal penalties as provided, and the relevant oversight bodies are empowered to investigate and take action.
