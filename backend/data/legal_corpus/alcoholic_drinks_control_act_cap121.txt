# Alcoholic Drinks Control Act (Cap. 121) — Kenya (key excerpts)

1. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Alcoholic Drinks Control Act.

Objects
The Act provides for the control of the production, sale, and consumption of alcoholic drinks, the promotion of temperance, and the protection of public health.

Licensing
No person shall manufacture, sell, or otherwise deal in alcoholic drinks except under and in accordance with a licence issued under this Act. Different classes of licence are provided for (e.g. manufacture, wholesale, retail, on-licence, off-licence).

Restrictions
The Act restricts the sale of alcoholic drinks to minors, the sale of alcoholic drinks in certain places or at certain times, and the advertising and promotion of alcoholic drinks in ways that target young persons or encourage excessive consumption.

Enforcement
Authorised officers have powers of inspection, search and seizure. Contravention of the Act attracts fines, imprisonment, and the possible cancellation or suspension of licences.

Note: There may be outstanding amendments (including Act No. 4 of 2023) that should be checked for the current position.
