# Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act (Cap. 254) — Kenya (key excerpts)

1. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act.

Objects
The Act provides for the prevention of the sale of unwholesome, adulterated or misbranded food, drugs and chemical substances, and for the regulation of their manufacture, importation, distribution and sale.

Prohibited acts
No person shall sell any food that is adulterated, unfit for human consumption, or labelled in a manner that is false, misleading or deceptive.
No person shall sell any drug that is adulterated or that does not comply with the prescribed standards of composition, strength, purity or quality.
No person shall sell any chemical substance in a manner that is likely to be injurious to health, or without the prescribed labelling and warnings.

Powers of authorised officers
Authorised officers may inspect premises, take samples, seize goods that appear to contravene the Act, and institute proceedings for offences under the Act.

Penalties
A person who contravenes the Act is liable on conviction to a fine or imprisonment or both, as prescribed. The court may also order the forfeiture of goods in respect of which an offence has been committed.
