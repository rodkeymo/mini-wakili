# Auctioneers Act (Cap. 526) — Kenya (key excerpts)

1. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Auctioneers Act.

Licensing
No person shall carry on the business of an auctioneer unless he is licensed under this Act. The Auctioneers Licensing Board is responsible for the licensing, regulation and discipline of auctioneers.

Duties of auctioneers
An auctioneer shall, in the conduct of an auction—
(a) act with integrity and in accordance with the law;
(b) disclose material information relevant to the sale;
(c) account for proceeds of sale in the manner prescribed;
(d) comply with any conditions attached to his licence and with rules made under the Act.

Attachment and sale of property
The Act regulates the manner in which auctioneers may attach and sell movable and immovable property in execution of court orders or other lawful authority, including requirements as to notice, valuation, and the conduct of the sale.

Discipline
The Board may suspend or revoke a licence, or impose other sanctions, where an auctioneer is found guilty of misconduct, including dishonesty, incompetence, or breach of the Act or regulations.
