# General Loan and Stock Act (Ordinance No. 14 of 1950) — Kenya (key excerpts)

Short title
This Ordinance may be cited as the General Loan and Stock Ordinance.

Object
An Ordinance to declare the terms and conditions applicable to loans authorized to be raised by the Government of the Colony and to provide for the creation of Kenya stock, and to enable the Colony to take advantage of the Colonial Stock Acts.

Loans to be raised by debentures or stock
Loans authorized to be raised by the Legislature may be raised by the issue of debentures or of Kenya stock, or partly by debentures and partly by stock, as the Governor may determine.

Loans to be a charge upon general revenue
The principal moneys and interest represented by any debentures or stock issued under this Ordinance are charged upon and payable out of the general revenues and assets of the Colony.

Borrowing upon debentures
The Governor may authorise the issue of debentures for the purpose of raising any loan. Debentures may be redeemed by annual drawings, by purchase in the market, or on a date fixed for redemption.

Interest and sinking fund
Provision is made for the payment of interest on debentures and stock, for the creation and application of sinking funds for the redemption of the principal, and for the appointment of trustees and the management of such funds.

Creation, inscription and transfer of stock
Kenya stock may be created, inscribed, registered, issued, converted and transferred in accordance with the provisions of the Ordinance and any regulations made under the Colonial Stock Act, 1877.
